To My Healer Friend

The Chapters You Never Knew You Wrote is my personal journey through heartbreak, physical pain, isolation, and the process of finding myself again. Through my friendship with Aradhya, I discovered lessons about healing, self-worth, and boundaries that changed how I see life. This isn't a love story, but a tribute to a friendship that made a difference without ever trying to. I wrote these chapters because some people change our lives without ever realising it, and I believe that gratitude deserves to be remembered.
I started with things I wanted to put into words. I ended up discovering a story of my own.

To My Healer Friend

Before meeting Aaradhya, I was carrying the silent weight of past heartbreaks, a severe back injury, and a growing sense of isolation that made me feel completely lost. A simple Instagram message and a mutual love for writing unexpectedly brought Aaradhya into my life, a girl whose quiet confidence and gothic aesthetic reminded me of Mavis. Through casual chats, reels, and honest conversations, she never tried to act like a savior, yet her perspective slowly challenged my self-doubt and taught me how to prioritize self-care. I found myself naturally drawn to going above and beyond for her—making handmade chocolates, buying jhumkas, and observing her fierce determination through her own life struggles. Even without a romantic label, she became my safe space, gently healing the parts of me I thought were permanently broken and proving that a true friend can change the entire direction of your life.

Wish I Had Stayed With You

After waking up with traumatic amnesia following a sudden accident, Raj struggles to piece together his forgotten past and the deep connection he shares with a mysterious girl. Surrounded by friends whose comforting lies hide a web of jealousy, manipulation, and heartbreaking sacrifices, he slowly begins to unearth the truth. Hand-crafted gifts, hidden diaries, and fragments of fading memories pull him closer to a love he fought to keep. Just as long-buried secrets finally come to light and old wounds begin to heal, life pulls them in opposite directions once again. Will love conquer the distance, or will they be left with nothing but memories and the haunting words, "I wish I had stayed with you"?

When Vihaan joins a new college with a dark and mysterious past, he immediately becomes the target of campus bullies led by the arrogant Rajveer.
