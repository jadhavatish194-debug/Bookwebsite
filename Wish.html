Behind "Wish I Had Stayed With You":

The Real Story of My First Book and How I Became a Writer.

There are moments in life when we look back and wonder what might have happened if we had made a different choice. A conversation we could have continued, a moment we could have held onto, or a person we wish we had understood better.

Sometimes, the things we leave behind stay with us longer than we expect. They become memories, questions, and feelings we struggle to put into words.

BEHIND THE PAGES

The Story Behind the Words

When readers ask me where the story came from, my answer is always a mix of truth and creativity. The emotional core of the book is pulled straight from real life. It is inspired by someone who once meant the world to me, a person who left an unforgettable mark on my heart and whose absence accidentally sparked my entire writing journey.

While the feelings, the heartbreak, and the nostalgia are rooted in my own past, I blended those real-life emotions with fictional elements and imagination to bring characters like Raj and Diya to life.

Sometimes, the hardest part isn't leaving. It's understanding what that moment meant to us.

Through this book, readers can explore themes of emotions, memories, relationships, and reflection. Everyone has a different story, but certain feelings connect us in ways that words alone cannot fully explain.

Why This Story Matters

Looking back, Wish I Had Stayed With You... carries the raw simplicity and imperfections of my early writing days. It explores themes we all secretly relate to: Unspoken words and missed chances. The weight of memories and first love. How one person can change the course of your life forever.

This book holds a permanent place in my heart because it was the first time I looked at a complete manuscript and realized, “I actually wrote a book.” It paved the way for everything I do today as a storyteller, author, and creative professional.

A Note to My Readers

To everyone who picks up this book: thank you. Whether you are reading it for the first time or revisiting the revised edition, you are sharing a piece of where my heart and career as an author truly began. I hope Raj and Diya's journey makes you feel, makes you reflect on your own unforgettable moments, and reminds you to cherish the people who touch your lives.

Perhaps the most meaningful part of a story is not only what the author writes, but also what the reader discovers within it.
